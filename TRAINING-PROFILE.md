Last updated 31 Oct 2026. My logged workouts and food are on my phone and
aren't in this file; I'll share screenshots or numbers when they're needed.

## About me

- Male, UK, metric units. I train at a David Lloyd: machines, cables,
  plate-loaded, Smith machine and dumbbells.
- Training since Jan 2023, consistently for the last ~12 months.
- 5 days a week, up to **1 hour** a session.
- I've lost ~30 kg overall (26 kg in 2026, mostly a big cut).
  Now ~103.9 kg, goal 95 kg.

## Rules for advice

- **Hypermobile.** Machines first, over barbells.
- **Never suggest squats or deadlifts.**
- **No dumbbell presses.** Dumbbell curls, lateral raises and rear delt flys
  are fine.
- Leg press and hip thrust at RPE 8–9, and never lock the knees. Isolation
  machines can go to RPE 9–10.
- Upper body is the priority. I don't love training legs, and they're already
  my best body part, so legs get one full day plus a top-up.
- I edit my routines myself. Give advice and reasons; don't rewrite my
  whole plan unless I ask.
- I get discouraged by day-to-day water-weight swings (salt, carbs, new
  training). Talk about the **trend**, not single weigh-ins. Don't suggest
  cutting calories based on a few days of data.
- Explain things in plain English.

## 12-week programme

Week 1 started on Mon 26 Oct 2026. There are three 4-week blocks. In each
block, weeks 1–3 build and week 4 is a deload.

| Block | Weeks | Reps | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Base | 1–4 | 10–15 | |
| 2 Build | 5–8 | 8–12 | +1 set on main lifts |
| 3 Strength | 9–12 | 5–8 on main lifts, 8–12 others | rest ≥150 s on main lifts; optional rep-max test in week 12 |

- **Deload weeks** start 16 Nov, 14 Dec and 11 Jan. They're ~10% lighter with
  half the sets, and calories go up to maintenance for that week
  (diet break).
- **Check-ins** are on Sun 22 Nov, Sun 20 Dec and Sun 17 Jan. Each records
  weight, waist, how I feel and a note. Sunday is a training day, so I weigh
  in before training.

## How my app progresses weights

- I rate the RPE of the last working set. The app estimates my 1RM from the
  last rated set: e1RM = weight × (1 + (reps + 10 − RPE) / 30).
- It then aims for ~1% a week growth. The target RPE for the last set is
  8, 8.5 and 9 in weeks 1–3 of each block.
- Without an RPE rating, it uses double progression: hit the top of the rep
  range on all sets, then go up one weight step.
- Weight steps: dumbbells 2 kg, machines 3–4 kg, barbell 2.5 kg.
- If there's no progress for 3 sessions in a row, it suggests a ~10% deload.

## Food

- Slow cut: aim ~0.25–0.5 kg a week.
- Targets: **2,400 kcal**, **170 g protein**, **70 g fat**, carbs the rest.
- Training days get ~+57 kcal and rest days ~−143 kcal, so the weekly total
  stays the same.
- A weekly check-in estimates my real maintenance from 3 weeks of intake plus
  the weight trend. It suggests changes of at most ±200 kcal, and I choose
  whether to accept.
- Don't eat back exercise calories, and ignore wearable calorie-burn figures.

## Recovery and wearables

- **Garmin Instinct 3**, worn 24/7 including in bed, syncs into my app. It
  tracks sleep (with stages), resting HR, Body Battery, stress, steps and gym
  sessions. HRV has been tracking only since late Oct 2026, so its baseline
  needs ~3 weeks. Training Readiness isn't available through the personal API.
- My app's readiness check flags any of these:
  - sleep under 6 h, or more than 1.25 h under my 7-day average
  - HRV low
  - resting HR at least 5 above normal
  - Body Battery under 30
- With no flags it says "Ready", one flag "Okay", and two or more "Take it
  easier". It's advice only.
- I also own an Apple Watch S10, a Fitbit and a Whoop. The Whoop
  subscription ends Mar 2027 and I've been advised not to renew.
- I don't have a Garmin scale. I weigh in using my app.
